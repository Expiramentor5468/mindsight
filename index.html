BLINDFOLD-FIRST DESIGN

Put the blindfold on once. The app runs the whole block.

Targets stay on screen until you answer. Voice listening is automatic by default. The app confirms, scores, advances, and ends the session without requiring you to remove the blindfold.

Session rule

Training can give immediate feedback. Challenge and Controlled Test do not reveal correctness until the block is complete.
