THE APPROACH

Open to possibility.
Honest about results.

MindSight is an original, experimental practice program. It draws instructional ideas from public lessons by Sean McNamara, Rob Freeman and Wendy Gallant, Dalia Burgoin, and Intuitu. It is not an official course from those teachers.

A practice companion

Settle, become familiar with known targets, then explore unknown ones. Describe freely, make a deliberate answer, hear feedback, and stay with the target if that helps. “Nothing yet” is a valid observation.

A clear record

Measurement blocks use one confirmed answer per target and delayed feedback. Passes, interruptions, and reported mask problems stay in the record. Above-chance results in a small block are not proof of perception without ordinary sight. Light leaks, cues, and chance must still be considered.

Comfort comes first

Practice seated with a comfortable mask. Breathe normally; there is no need to strain your eyes, hold your breath, use flashing lights, or walk while blindfolded. Check suspected light leaks instead of assuming they are progress.

Voice & privacy

Zen and Firefox use on-device English speech recognition. Its engine and model download from jsDelivr and the Vosk Browser project; recognition audio stays on your device. Browsers with built-in recognition use their speech provider by default and may send audio to that provider. Select On-device to use local recognition instead. MindSight has no account, analytics, or app server and records microphone audio only when you opt in. Recordings, session notes and results stay in this browser unless you export them. Local voice services receive guide text only when you configure and select them. Clearing site data removes them.

Speech support varies. On-device recognition needs an initial download of about 46 MB including its engine and enough available device memory. The app verifies a spoken phrase each session. Speak after the guide finishes and the indicator says “Listening.” During prompts, use the visible Pause button or Escape key to interrupt immediately.

Download on-device recognition?

Before voice recognition can start, MindSight will download an English speech model (about 40 MB) plus its engine (about 6 MB) from the Vosk Browser project and jsDelivr. Loading may take a few minutes. Your browser may cache these files; a future visit may download them again.

No microphone access is requested during this download. Recognition runs on this device after loading. This option is intended for compatible Zen, Firefox, Chrome and Edge browsers with microphone, Web Audio and WebAssembly support; the spoken check verifies your setup.
